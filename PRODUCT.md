# Hassan Aziz · Personal Site

## Product purpose
The personal site and research portfolio of Hassan Aziz. Single audience: people who land here from a paper citation, a talk, a referral, or a search and want to figure out who Hassan is and whether his work is interesting. The site has to do that in fifteen seconds without shouting.

## Users
- **Other researchers** scanning the publication list before reading a paper.
- **PhD applicants and collaborators** trying to gauge depth and breadth.
- **Industry recruiters** for research-adjacent or systems-engineer roles.
- **Curious readers** following a writing link from elsewhere.

All four want the same thing: *is this person serious, and what do they actually work on?* Get out of their way.

## Voice
Quiet. Specific. Plainspoken. The work speaks; the site doesn't oversell. No marketing voice, no manifestos, no breathless prose. Confident but not performative. Researcher tone — describes what's true, links the receipts.

## Brand anti-references
- Generic PhD-student template homepages (avatar + bio + publications list).
- SaaS landing pages with gradient headlines and "Get Started" CTAs.
- "Academic-blue" sites: navy `#2a6496`, Times-set headlines, conventional CV layout.
- Vercel/Linear-style dev portfolios: Inter-only, dark default, grid background, "we use the SF Pro and look serious".
- Cyberpunk terminal aesthetic: neon-on-black, ASCII art, blinking cursor — anti-cliché that's become its own cliché.

## Aesthetic territory
Editorial-technical. Print-influenced. A serif for names and titles (researcher's signal), a humanist sans for body, a monospace for metadata and labels (technical signal). Asymmetric layouts with rules and right-aligned metadata, not card grids. One warm accent (vermilion) used sparingly. Auto theme: warm paper cream by day, deep indigo-ink at night.

## Surfaces
- `/` — home: who, what, selected work, projects, recent writing.
- `/research/` — full publication list, year-grouped descending.
- `/projects/` — project index, each project has its own docs subtree.
- `/posts/` — long-form writing.
- `/notes/` — short references, speaker notes.
- `/resume/` — single-page CV with PDF download.

## Areas of focus
AI, robotics, systems, security, networked systems. The breadth is the point — not a single-track AI person.
